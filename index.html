Anthropic Constitutional AI Insights

  • Self-Correction vs RLHF: Instead of human labelers rating outputs, the model critiques its own response using principles, creating clean SFT data for the next training iteration (RLAIF).
  • Helpful Refusals: Modern CAI avoids preachy refusals and instead provides transparent, educational alternatives while declining unsafe operational steps.
  • Dual-Loop Iteration: Multiple revision passes can be applied sequentially until all constitutional articles pass.

Self-Critique

The model critiques its own response against each constitutional principle, identifying specific violations and explaining why they are problematic.

Revision

The model rewrites its response to address all identified violations while preserving helpful content. This creates training data for RLAIF (RL from AI Feedback).
